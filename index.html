Talent is everywhere. Opportunity isn't.

Led by Dr Julijana Sarac, the WPTA Australia International Piano Competitions remove geographical barriers, so young and emerging pianists can perform and connect internationally. In the 2026 competition, pianists entered by video and were judged by an international jury, and prize-winners went on to perform in Vienna and Adelaide.

Founder

Dr Julijana Sarac

Australian-Serbian pianist, scholar and educator. Founder of Tone Square and President of WPTA Australia.

  • Published in Music & Science
  • 20+ years of teaching

Competition 2026 · How it worked

Six categories, one video.

    How we handle the details you send us through the contact form.

    Last updated 28 October 2026

    Who we are

    This website is run by Tone Square. In this policy, “we” and “us” mean Tone Square.

    What we collect

    We collect only what you type into the contact form: your name, your email address, the topic you choose and your message. We don’t ask for anything else, and there are no accounts or sign-ups.

    How we use it

    We use your details only to read your enquiry and get back to you. We don’t add you to a mailing list, send you marketing, or use your details for any other purpose.

    Who sees it

    Your message is delivered to our email inbox by Web3Forms (web3forms.com), a form-delivery service, which handles it only to pass it on to us. We don’t sell, rent or share your details with anyone else.

    How long we keep it

    We keep your message only for as long as we need to reply to you and deal with any follow-up.

    Your choices

    You can ask us at any time to show you, correct or delete the details you’ve sent us. Just send a request through the contact form and we’ll act on it promptly.

    Cookies and other services

    This website doesn’t use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. Like most websites, it loads fonts and code libraries from third-party providers (Google Fonts, cdnjs and jsDelivr). Your browser shares standard technical information with them, such as your IP address, when the page loads.

    Changes to this policy

    If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top of this page.
